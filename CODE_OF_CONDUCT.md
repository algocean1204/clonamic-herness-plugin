# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss the code and the observed behavior, not the person reporting it. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and publication of another person's private data are not accepted.

Report conduct problems privately through the maintainer contact listed in the repository security or profile information. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

This short policy may be replaced by the Contributor Covenant as the community grows.
